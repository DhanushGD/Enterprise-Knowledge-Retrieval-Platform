# Internal: SLA tiers and response targets

This page is for support staff only.

| Plan | First response | Resolution target |
|------|----------------|-------------------|
| Free | 48 hours | Best effort |
| Pro | 8 hours | 3 business days |
| Enterprise | 1 hour | 1 business day |

Enterprise tickets marked **urgent** page the on-call engineer after 30 minutes without a response.
