# Refund policy for subscriptions

Customers on monthly plans can request a refund within 14 days of a charge.
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.

## Eligibility
- The charge must be the customer's first charge for that plan, or an unintended renewal.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days.

## How to issue a refund
1. Open the invoice in the billing console.
2. Choose **Refund** and select full or partial.
3. Add the support ticket number in the notes field.

## Not refundable
- Usage-based overage charges.
- Charges older than the refund window, unless approved by a team lead.
