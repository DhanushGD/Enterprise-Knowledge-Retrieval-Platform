# Resetting a customer's password

OpenDesk accounts use email-based password reset. Agents never see or set passwords.

## Steps
1. Confirm the customer's identity using the verification checklist.
2. Open the customer record and choose **Send reset link**.
3. The link expires after 30 minutes and works once.

## If the email does not arrive
- Ask the customer to check spam and the address on file.
- Wait 5 minutes before sending another link; repeated sends are rate limited.
- If the account uses SSO, the customer must reset through their identity provider instead.

## Escalation
If the customer is locked out after three failed resets, escalate to the security team.
